void phone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

void phone is a minimal home screen (launcher) app for Android. It shows the apps you choose as circles on a black background and opens them when you tap.

Data collection

void phone does not collect, sell or share personal data. It does not require an account, does not include advertising, does not use analytics, and does not send any information to the developer or to third parties. It has no internet permission, so it cannot transmit data.

Information used on your device

To work as a home screen, void phone reads the list of apps installed on your device (their names and icons) so it can display and open them. If you choose an icon pack, it reads that pack's icons and app mappings. This information is used only on your device and never leaves it.

Your favourite apps, layout, colour theme, terminal header text and icon pack choice are saved only on your device. If you use Android's own device backup, these settings may be included in your personal backup, which you control in your device settings.

App permissions

void phone does not request any permissions that need your approval. It has no access to the internet, your location, contacts, files, camera or microphone.

Third-party services

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google Play may process download, device or account information according to Google's own policies. Those practices are controlled by Google, not by void phone.

Children's privacy

void phone is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's behaviour changes. Any material change will be reflected by updating this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact on the Google Play listing or open an issue at github.com/angusm99/void-phone-launcher/issues.
